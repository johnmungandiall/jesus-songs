STYLE: 03 — Devi Sri Prasad Folk Janapada Dance
GOOD FOR: Village outreach, festival praise, harvest thanksgiving
TEMPO: 132 BPM (upbeat)

=== SUNO STYLE PROMPT ===
Telugu Christian janapada folk-dance song in Devi Sri Prasad Pushpa "Saami Saami" tradition with Telugu vocals, raw rustic female lead with male folk chorus chants, upbeat tempo around 132 BPM, dappu, dholak, talam claps, nadaswaram, harmonium, ghatam, and folk flute, earthy village-festival janapada tone.
